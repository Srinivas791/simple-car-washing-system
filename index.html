Environmental Stewardship

Clean Cars. Pure Water.
85% Water Reclaimed on Site.

A standard driveway car wash uses up to 100 gallons of potable municipal water and washes harsh detergents into municipal storm drains. HydroShine uses closed-loop multi-stage cyclonic filtration and reverse osmosis to recycle 85% of wash runoff.

100% Biodegradable Surfactants

Gentle on ceramic paintwork and completely non-toxic to aquatic biology.

Reverse Osmosis De-ionization

Removes dissolved minerals (TDS < 5 ppm) for a guaranteed spot-free finish without abrasive hand wiping.

Solar-Powered High Velocity Blowers

Our warm-air drying tunnels operate on 40kW rooftop solar arrays, reducing carbon emissions.
